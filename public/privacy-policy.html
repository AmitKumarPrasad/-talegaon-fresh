Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FRESHORA, the customer ordering service operated under the Talegaon Fresh brand, handles information when you use our website or mobile application.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Account information: name and mobile number used to create and access your customer account.
  • Delivery information: addresses and related delivery details that you provide for an order.
  • Order information: products, quantities, order totals, order status and order history.
  • Customer support and AI assistant messages: messages you send through the FRESHORA assistant, including information you choose to include in those messages.
  • Technical information: information needed to operate authentication, security, diagnostics and reliable API communication.

2. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your customer account.
  • To process, confirm and deliver orders.
  • To show your order history and status.
  • To respond to customer support and AI assistant requests.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot service problems.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. AI assistant and third-party processing

When you use the FRESHORA AI assistant, your message may be processed by our backend and by third-party AI infrastructure used to provide the requested response. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information in chat.

The AI assistant is not the authoritative source for product prices, inventory, payment status or order totals. Those values are determined by the application backend.

4. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers that are necessary to operate the service, such as cloud hosting, database, authentication, messaging or AI infrastructure providers. We do not sell customer personal information.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, fraud prevention or protection of the rights and safety of customers and the service.

5. Data retention

We retain account, address and order information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain order records, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods may vary by data type.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so please use care when sharing information online.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security or transaction-record purposes.

8. Children's privacy

FRESHORA is intended for customers who can lawfully use the service and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for independent account creation.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, technology or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Talegaon Fresh through WhatsApp at +91 87885 43135 or use our Contact page.
