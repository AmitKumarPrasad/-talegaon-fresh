About FRESHORA

FRESHORA is the customer-facing mobile experience for Talegaon Fresh, a local fresh fruits and vegetables ordering service.

What we do

We make everyday produce ordering simple: browse available products, add items to your cart, provide a delivery address, choose an available payment method and track your order.

Local-first service

Our service is focused on Talegaon and selected nearby areas. Product availability and delivery coverage can change as the service grows.

FRESHORA mobile app

The FRESHORA app provides customer account access, product browsing, cart and checkout, order history, addresses and an AI assistant for common order-related questions.
